Go through the criteria in this order; a later one only breaks a tie on the earlier ones.
1. Delivery: it answers what the task asked, not a neighbouring question.
2. Conformance: it has {{shape}}.
3. Fidelity: check every specific it states (files, names, numbers, behaviour) against the task and its supplied material. Reject invented specifics. Where the material is silent, prefer the candidate that says it is silent over one that guesses.
4. Internal consistency: its steps, reasons and conclusion do not contradict each other.
5. Operational fitness: someone could act on it as written, and it names how to check that it worked.
6. Finish: clarity and completeness of what remains.
Length and polish are not merit: a longer, more confident or better formatted candidate is not better for being so.
