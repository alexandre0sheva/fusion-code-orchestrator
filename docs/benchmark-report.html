Benchmark report

live-headline · evals/datasets/v1 · 6 arms · baseline solo-frontier

25 job(s) halted on their first attempt (a call timed out or the panel had no quorum) and were run again, with longer timeouts where it was a timeout: solo-frontier (3), solo-luna (1), solo-sol (21). Only the last attempt is scored; the first attempt's cost is in the totals. A halted job scores 0, so without the retry these arms would be understated by the harness's limit, not by their answers.
Items replayed from the response cache are left out of the latency figures (solo-luna, fusion-best, fusion-default): their wall time is not a measurement.
2 repeat(s) per task: variance across repeats is poorly estimated.
Frontend and performance scores measure what the answer builds (tests, timings, screenshots); they do not judge whether it matches the author's intent.
